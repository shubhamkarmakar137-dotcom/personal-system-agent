Nobel Prize Winners in Science (2020 - 2024)

==================================================
2024
==================================================
- Physiology or Medicine:
  * Victor Ambros and Gary Ruvkun
    Reason: Discovery of microRNA and its role in post-transcriptional gene regulation.

- Physics:
  * John J. Hopfield and Geoffrey E. Hinton
    Reason: Foundational discoveries and inventions that enable machine learning with artificial neural networks.

- Chemistry:
  * David Baker
    Reason: Computational protein design.
  * Demis Hassabis and John M. Jumper
    Reason: Protein structure prediction (AlphaFold).

==================================================
2023
==================================================
- Physiology or Medicine:
  * Katalin Karikó and Drew Weissman
    Reason: Discoveries concerning nucleoside base modifications that enabled effective mRNA vaccines against COVID-19.

- Physics:
  * Pierre Agostini, Ferenc Krausz, and Anne L'Huillier
    Reason: Experimental methods generating attosecond pulses of light for the study of electron dynamics in matter.

- Chemistry:
  * Moungi G. Bawendi, Louis E. Brus, and Alexei I. Ekimov
    Reason: Discovery and synthesis of quantum dots.

==================================================
2022
==================================================
- Physiology or Medicine:
  * Svante Pääbo
    Reason: Discoveries concerning the genomes of extinct hominins and human evolution.

- Physics:
  * Alain Aspect, John F. Clauser, and Anton Zeilinger
    Reason: Experiments with entangled photons, establishing the violation of Bell inequalities, and pioneering quantum information science.

- Chemistry:
  * Carolyn R. Bertozzi, Morten Meldal, and K. Barry Sharpless
    Reason: Development of click chemistry and bioorthogonal chemistry.

==================================================
2021
==================================================
- Physiology or Medicine:
  * David Julius and Ardem Patapoutian
    Reason: Discoveries of receptors for temperature and touch.

- Physics:
  * Syukuro Manabe and Klaus Hasselmann
    Reason: Physical modelling of Earth's climate, quantifying variability, and reliably predicting global warming.
  * Giorgio Parisi
    Reason: Discovery of the interplay of disorder and fluctuations in physical systems from atomic to planetary scales.

- Chemistry:
  * Benjamin List and David W.C. MacMillan
    Reason: Development of asymmetric organocatalysis.

==================================================
2020
==================================================
- Physiology or Medicine:
  * Harvey J. Alter, Michael Houghton, and Charles M. Rice
    Reason: Discovery of the Hepatitis C virus.

- Physics:
  * Roger Penrose
    Reason: Discovery that black hole formation is a robust prediction of the general theory of relativity.
  * Reinhard Genzel and Andrea Ghez
    Reason: Discovery of a supermassive compact object at the center of our galaxy.

- Chemistry:
  * Emmanuelle Charpentier and Jennifer A. Doudna
    Reason: Development of a method for genome editing (CRISPR-Cas9).
